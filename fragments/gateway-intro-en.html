Chat platforms hand you webhooks and send APIs — not conversation lanes, pairing, or delivery guarantees. Platform adapters own the wire: verify callbacks, normalize platform payloads into MessageEvent, and send replies when the gateway asks. The gateway is the shared bot layer: session_key lanes, authorization, slash commands, busy queues and interrupts, and the delivery ledger that retries outbound text. The Hermes CLI bypasses the gateway but runs the same AIAgent turn loop; every messenger path goes through adapters plus GatewayRunner. Without that layer, you reimplement session routing, auth, and delivery for each platform you add.
