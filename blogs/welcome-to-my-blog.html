Welcome to My Blog

August 23, 2026

Hey, I'm Daniel. I finally carved out a corner of my personal site for writing. This is the first post — less a deep dive, more a note about what this space is for.

What I'll write about

A loose mix of things I'm building and learning:

  • Engineering — frameworks, system design, things I break and fix.
  • AI — agents, LLM tooling, and the practical edges of the field.
  • Projects — post-mortems on the stuff in my Projects section.

Why a blog here

My site was a portfolio template — the "Blog" tab existed but pointed at nothing. So I wired up a real blog: each post is a self-contained HTML page that reuses the site's theme, and it's all served by GitHub Pages with zero build step.

That means posting is just writing a file and pushing. Easy enough that I might actually keep it up.

More soon. Thanks for reading.
